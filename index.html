01. Plant Efficiency Dashboard

Problem
Daily efficiency reporting relied on macro-heavy Excel files rebuilt by hand, causing data latency and bottlenecks.
Solution
A Python pipeline generates one self-contained page with standard vs actual hours, efficiency against budget, and € variance, from daily to YTD.
Result
A plant meeting opens one instantly-loading link instead of downloading a spreadsheet.

03. Extra Consumptions Tracker

Problem
Scrap and extra picking costs are often buried inside the total material variance, making root-cause analysis difficult.
Solution
Total variance is mathematically split into scrap, extra picks, and pure consumption by material category, with expandable rows down to individual movements.
Result
It is immediately clear which categories drive overconsumption and by how many euros.
